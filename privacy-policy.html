Privacy Policy — Enter 50 Contests Free (Contest Links Weekly)

Effective November 4, 2026. Operator: Paul Digby, Sooke, BC, Canada. Contact: 24hourcomputers@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Email address you enter to sign up, and whether you agreed to receive our newsletter.
  • Entry tracker data: which contest links you mark as entered, dates, wins you log, and settings.

Where it is stored

Currently all data is stored only on your device (browser local storage). No analytics or advertising trackers are used. If we add an account server, your email and entry count will be stored there to apply the 50 free entries and billing, and this policy will be updated.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Payment processors receive what they need to process payment.

Why we collect it (purpose) and consent

We collect your email only to give you your account and free-entry allowance and, if you tick the separate box, to send the weekly newsletter. This policy follows Canada's PIPEDA and BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). You can withdraw consent at any time. Marketing emails are sent only with your express consent under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), identify the sender with a mailing address, and include a working unsubscribe link honoured within 10 business days.

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing 24hourcomputers@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. The country, province/state and language you pick are stored only on your device to filter contests and translate the app. We do not use GPS or IP location.

Delete your account
